Privacy Policy for Amortisation Calculator

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Amortisation Calculator app ("the App") handles user information.

Information We Collect

To generate a loan amortisation schedule, the App asks you to enter:

  • Your email address
  • Loan details you provide: principal amount, interest rate, loan period, and inception date

This information is sent to our backend server each time you calculate a schedule, so that the server can perform the calculation and return your results.

How We Use This Information

Your email address is used to identify the request when calculating your amortisation schedule. Your loan details are used solely to compute the repayment figures and schedule shown to you in the App. We do not use this information for marketing, and we do not sell it to third parties.

Data Storage and Retention

The Amortisation Calculator does not permanently store users' calculation data on our servers. Information submitted through the app, including an email address and financing or loan calculation details, may be transmitted securely to our server to process the requested calculation and generate the results. The submitted information is not retained in a permanent database after the calculation has been processed, except where temporary technical logs or records may be retained for a limited period for security, troubleshooting, and service operation purposes.

Third-Party Services

The App does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Data you submit is sent only to our own backend server described above.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you submit. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your email address and associated data from our records.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact: admin@wird.co.za
